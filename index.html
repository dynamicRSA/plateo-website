Our Story

Madrid's Kitchen,
Your Event

Pla-téo is a Spanish-owned catering business specialising in Authentic Spanish Street Food, learnt from the heart of a family kitchen in Madrid. Spanish cuisine is all about socialising and enjoying food at the same time.

Sarita, as a proud Spaniard, aims to share authentic Spanish flavours and experiences with dishes she grew up with — getting inspiration from her dad's cooking, who taught her to be proud of her culture and to cook with love.

"Add some passion to your food-life with our authentic Spanish taste."

Booking Conditions

Terms & Conditions

Please read these carefully before confirming your booking. Payment of the deposit constitutes your acceptance of all terms below.

01

Our Service Model

Pla-téo operates exclusively as a private on-site catering service. We arrive at your chosen venue, set up our equipment, and cook fresh for your guests. We are not a restaurant, a food truck, or a delivery service.

02

Minimum Guest Count

Private catering events require a minimum of 40 guests. Each paella variety requires a minimum of 20 portions. For groups under 100, we recommend limiting selection to two types to ensure the best possible quality and presentation.

03

Service Duration & Overtime

Our quoted fee covers a service window up to 1.5 hours from the confirmed start time. For each additional hour, or part thereof, beyond this window, an overtime rate of R100 per person on-site will be charged.

Where the commencement of service is delayed at the client’s request, or due to circumstances within the client’s control, the original agreed serving time remains the reference point for calculating when overtime begins.

04

Payment Schedule

A deposit of 50% of the total invoice value is required to secure your booking date. No date is held until this deposit is received.

The remaining balance (50%) is due no later than 7 calendar days before the event. Late payment may result in your booking being released.

For late enquiries — bookings confirmed less than 7 days before the event date — where Pla-téo is able to accommodate, full payment (100%) is required upfront before any preparations are commenced.

05

Cutlery & Crockery

Cutlery, crockery, serving dishes, and related tableware are not included in our standard quote unless explicitly listed as a line item. Please discuss your requirements with us when requesting a quote.

06

Travel & Transport

Transport to and from your venue is calculated individually based on distance, access logistics, and equipment requirements. Travel costs are detailed separately in your quote and are payable as part of the total invoice.

07

Quotes & Validity

All quotes issued by Pla-téo are valid for 5 business days from the date of issue. Prices are subject to change after this period, particularly where ingredient costs or logistics have shifted.

08

Liability

Pla-téo will not be held liable for any loss, damage, or injury caused by or relating to our catering service. We commit to exercising all reasonable care and professional diligence to ensure the highest standard of service at your event.

09

Cancellation Policy

The 50% booking deposit is non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.

Should a cancellation be received within 7 days of the event, or after the balance payment has been settled, the full invoice amount remains payable.

Pla-téo reserves the right to cancel a booking in exceptional or unforeseen circumstances. In such cases, all payments received will be refunded in full.

By paying your deposit, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agree to all of the above terms and conditions.
