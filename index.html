Our Story

Madrid's Kitchen,
Your Event

Pla-téo is a Spanish-owned catering business specialising in Authentic Spanish Street Food, learnt from the heart of a family kitchen in Madrid. Spanish cuisine is all about socialising and enjoying food at the same time.

Sarita, as a proud Spaniard, aims to share authentic Spanish flavours and experiences with dishes she grew up with — getting inspiration from her dad's cooking, who taught her to be proud of her culture and to cook with love.

"Add some passion to your food-life with our authentic Spanish taste."
